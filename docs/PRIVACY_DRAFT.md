## 5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption**: Data encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access and authentication
- **Regular Audits**: Security assessments and penetration testing
- **Monitoring**: 24/7 security monitoring and incident response
- **Backup**: Regular data backups with disaster recovery plans

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide the Services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy:

- **Account Data**: Retained while your account is active and for 2 years after account closure
- **Farm Data**: Retained for the duration of your subscription and for 5 years thereafter (for agricultural trend analysis)
- **Images**: Retained for 3 years or until you request deletion
- **Usage Logs**: Retained for 1 year
- **Legal Records**: Retained as required by law

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at privacy@agrovision.app.
